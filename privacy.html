Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Versión en español

QA Path ("the app") is an app for learning software testing through lessons, quizzes and interview questions. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information the app handles and what it doesn't.

Summary

QA Path does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. You don't need to create an account. Your progress is stored on your device, using iOS local storage (UserDefaults), and we never see it.

What is stored on your device

  • The lessons you've completed.
  • The automation track you choose (for example, Playwright with TypeScript).
  • The date until which your QA Path Premium purchase is valid, so the app recognizes it at launch even if the App Store is slow to respond. This date is stored in the iOS keychain.

The app doesn't send any of this to our servers or to third parties. If you back up your device (to iCloud or a computer), your progress and track may be included in those backups and come back when you restore them. The expiration date isn't transferred to another device.

How to delete your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my progress removes the completed lessons and the chosen track on this device.
  • If you uninstall the app, iOS deletes your progress from this device. The Premium expiration date may remain in the keychain so your purchase is recognized if you reinstall. Copies already in your backups are managed in your iCloud or computer settings.

Because we have no access to your information, we can't view, correct, or delete it from any server. Even so, you can write to us with any request about your personal data (access, correction, deletion, or objection) and we will respond.

In-app purchases (QA Path Premium)

QA Path Premium purchases are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit) using your Apple Account. We don't receive or store your payment information; Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Each lesson includes links to its sources (for example, official documentation). If you tap one, it opens in your browser and that site's privacy policy applies.

No ads, no tracking

QA Path doesn't show third-party ads, doesn't use analytics or ad-tracking tools, and doesn't share information with social networks or advertisers.

Children

QA Path is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

This website

These legal pages don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking tools.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Controller and contact

QA Path is operated by Omar Mascorro Godinez (independent developer, Mexico). If you have questions about this policy or your data, write to us at omascorro.apps@gmail.com.
